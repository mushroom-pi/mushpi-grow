Provisioning mode — view-only

This unit is waiting for Wi-Fi credentials. While it waits:
  • The relays are OFF and stay off — the control loop is not running.
  • Every control on this page is locked: the unit refuses changes (HTTP 403) until it has joined a network.
How to leave: open the Wi-Fi setup form (the unit's root address), enter your network credentials and submit. The unit saves them, reboots, and joins the network — this page then unlocks. If GP0 was held LOW at boot to force provisioning: free GP0 and power-cycle the unit instead.

Reboot

Restart the unit. A soft reboot is faster and preserves cumulative uptime; a hard reboot is a full power-cycle.
 
Soft — machine.soft_reset(): restarts the program, keeps cumulative uptime, reconnects Wi-Fi and re-announces to the hub. Hard — machine.reset(): full power-cycle, uptime counters reset to zero. Either way the unit is unreachable for about a minute and relays are forced safe during the restart.
Served by the unit itself — no sign-in, no internet access, local network only.
Raw data lives at / (JSON) and the sub-endspoints /system · /health · /sensors · /outputs · /control · /setpoints · /setup.
